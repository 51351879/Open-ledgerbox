What was spent, by category

The hatched slice is not a category: it is the lines no rule claimed and nobody overruled. It is drawn differently, listed by name, and never called "other" — a catch-all bucket is how a breakdown that covers a third of a ledger comes to look complete. Small slices are drawn small; nothing here is inflated to be visible, which is what the list beside the chart is for. A legend percentage is the share of spending amount, not the share of transactions. When a bucket is switched off, the visible wedges and visible percentages are rebalanced against the visible spending; the full-ledger coverage statement below the chart still reports both amount and line count.

Transactions

Every booked line, worded exactly as the bank printed it. A category shown as — is a line no rule claimed, and there is no catch-all bucket for it to fall into: it stays empty rather than being swept into an "other" that would look, in any chart, exactly like a category something matched on purpose. Each row says whether a rule or a person answered. The three figures under the controls are the bank leg — what these lines did to this account's balance, transfers included. The four at the top of the page read the income and expense legs instead, and leave out transfers and the opening balance. Different questions, so the two line up only while this list holds the lines those four count — which it does before you filter anything, on a ledger with no transfers marked.
